FOR MY MADAM JI ♡

Meri Pyari Madam Ji 🎀❤️

Dear Madam Ji, meri pyari Malkin ❤️👑🫶🏻

Sabse pehle, main tumse dil se sorry kehna chahta hoon. Mujhe pata hai ki mujhse bahut si galtiyan hui hain. Kabhi-kabhi main bahut zyada overthink karne lagta hoon, possessive aur overprotective ho jaata hoon, aur apni feelings samjhate-samjhate aisi baatein keh deta hoon jo tumhe hurt karti hain. Main apni galtiyon ke liye koi bahana nahi banana chahta. Mujhe sach mein afsos hai ki meri wajah se tumhe takleef hui. 🥺❤️‍🩹

Jab tumne mujhe milne ke liye bulaya tha aur main nahi aa paaya, toh mujhe tumhari feelings ko aur achhe se samajhna chahiye tha. Main chahta tha ki hum apne career aur relationship ko saath lekar chalein, lekin shayad apni baat samjhane ki koshish mein main tumhari baat dhyaan se sunna bhool gaya. Mujhe samajhna chahiye tha ki tumhari feelings aur tumhari expectations bhi utni hi important hain. 💗

September se lekar ab tak, agar meri kisi baat, gusse ya behaviour ne tumhara dil dukhaya hai, toh uske liye main dil se maafi maangta hoon. Main beete hue waqt ko badal nahi sakta, lekin apni galtiyon ko samajhkar khud ko behtar banane ki koshish zaroor kar sakta hoon. Main seekhna chahta hoon ki gusse mein kaise rukna hai, overthinking ko kaise control karna hai aur tumhari baat ko bina kisi behas ke kaise sunna hai. ❤️‍🩹🫂

Aur baby, aaj jo mujhse galti hui, uske liye bhi main tumse dil se maafi maangta hoon. Mere muh se tumhare liye gaali nikal gayi, aur mujhe khud par bahut regret ho raha hai. Yrr, sach kahun toh main tumhe kabhi gaali dena toh door, aisa sochna bhi nahi chahta. Lekin jo words mere muh se nikle, unse tumhe kitna bura laga hoga, main samajh sakta hoon. Main neend mein tha ya meri condition kuch bhi thi, lekin isse meri galti chhoti nahi ho jaati. Tumhe hurt hua, aur uski responsibility meri hai. 🥺

Baby, please ho sake toh mujhe maaf kar dena. Main dil se keh raha hoon ki aage se apne words aur gusse par control rakhne ki poori koshish karunga. Main sirf promise nahi karna chahta, balki apne behaviour se tumhe ye dikhana chahta hoon ki main sach mein khud ko better bana raha hoon. ❤️

Madam Ji, tum mere liye bahut special ho. Tumhari smile, tumhari khushi aur tumhara sukoon mere liye bahut maayne rakhte hain. 👑🎀 Tumse bahut pyaar karta hoon, lekin main ye bhi samajhta hoon ki pyaar ka matlab sirf care karna nahi, balki trust, understanding, respect aur ek-doosre ko space dena bhi hota hai.

Yrr, tujhe pata hai na main tujhse kitna pyaar karta hoon. Kabhi-kabhi gusse mein bol deta hoon "chhod de, chhod de", lekin sach toh ye hai ki main kabhi nahi chahta ki humara rishta kharab ho. Tu meri life ka bahut special hissa hai, baby. Main tujhe khona nahi chahta. ❤️

Pata nahi mere saath hi aisa kyun hota hai. Main kuch achha karne ki koshish karta hoon aur jaane-anjaane mein sab kharab kar deta hoon. Main perfect nahi hoon, mujhse galtiyan hoti hain, lekin main apni galtiyon ko samajhna aur khud ko badalna chahta hoon. Sirf tere liye nahi, balki humare rishte ko aur achha banane ke liye bhi. 🥹

Ek baat aur baby, main tujhse ek request karna chahta hoon. Please hum dono roz-roz ke kalesh thode kam karte hain na. Pehle bhi tu mujhse ladti thi, chhoti-chhoti baaton par naraz hoti thi, lekin tab un baaton mein bhi ek alag sa pyaar mehsoos hota tha. Teri narazgi bhi mujhe pyaari lagti thi. Main chahta hoon ki hum phir se ek-doosre ke saath waise hi khush rahein. 💗

Main teri baatein sunta hoon aur teri narazgi samajhne ki koshish karta hoon, lekin baby, main bhi insaan hoon. Kabhi-kabhi kuch baaton se mujhe bhi takleef hoti hai. Main nahi chahta ki hum gusse mein ek-doosre se aisi baatein keh dein jo baad mein hum dono ko hurt karein.

Tu naraz ho, mujhse apni har baat keh, mujh par gussa bhi kar, lekin please hum dono ek-doosre ki respect kabhi mat khona. Main bhi ye poori koshish karunga ki apne words ya behaviour se tujhe kabhi hurt na karun. Hum dono ko ek-doosre ko samajhne ki koshish karni chahiye, na ki har baar ek-doosre ko galat saabit karna chahiye. ❤️🫂

Main sirf sorry bolkar sab kuch theek hone ki umeed nahi rakhna chahta. Main apne actions se behtar insaan banna chahta hoon. Main jaanta hoon ki trust dobara banne mein waqt lag sakta hai, aur main is baat ko samajhta hoon. Tumhari feelings, tumhara sukoon aur tumhara decision mere liye hamesha respect ke layak hain. 🥹🌷

Please yaar, agar kabhi tumhare dil mein mere liye maafi ki jagah ho, toh mujhe maaf kar dena. 😭❤️ Main tumhe khona nahi chahta, lekin main tum par mujhe maaf karne ya mere saath rehne ka pressure bhi nahi daalna chahta. Agar tumhe waqt aur space chahiye, toh main uski bhi respect karunga.

Main tumse bas itna kehna chahta hoon ki jo kuch bhi hua, uske liye mujhe dil se afsos hai. Kaash main pehle hi apni galtiyon ko samajh paata aur tumhe hurt karne se khud ko rok paata. Main apni galtiyon ki responsibility leta hoon aur khud mein sachcha badlaav laane ki koshish karunga.

Agar kabhi tum khud mujhse baat karna chaho, toh main tumhari baat calmly sununga aur tumhari feelings ko samajhne ki poori koshish karunga. Main chahta hoon ki humare beech pyaar ke saath-saath dosti, bharosa aur respect bhi hamesha bana rahe. ❤️🦋

Apna khayal rakhna, Madam Ji. Hamesha khush rehna aur apni pyari si smile banaye rakhna. 🎀🌸

Ek baar phir, meri taraf se dil se sorry. Main perfect nahi hoon, lekin apni galtiyon ko pehchaan kar ek behtar insaan aur behtar partner banna chahta hoon.

I am so sorry, baby. ❤️
Miss you so much, Babu. 🥺
I love you so much, Garima. ❤️💗💓

Tumhara pagal sa insaan ❤️🥹🫶🏻
Always wishing you happiness ♡
